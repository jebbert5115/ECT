Privacy policy

Edgenuity Client Tweaks · Version 1.3.0

This independent extension customizes the appearance and audio of supported Edgenuity pages. It is not affiliated with Edgenuity or Imagine Learning.

Data processed

The extension examines page elements and styling locally to apply the theme, identify media and optionally identify a video feedback survey. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share page content, student records, credentials, browsing history or personal information. It contains no analytics or remote code.

Local storage

Theme choice, brightness, mute and survey preferences are stored using the browser's extension-local storage. They remain on the device and can be reset in the popup or removed by uninstalling the extension.

Permissions

Storage access saves preferences. Site access on Edgenuity, Imagine Learning and Education2020 domains allows the extension to customize pages and supported embedded frames. No account access is requested.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the publisher through the extension's Chrome Web Store support listing.
